# Page 12 — Shrine references

The six side shrines, their placements, and the themed fortune verses are original game content.

- Wealth and commerce, Ebisu (Hiruko Ōkami). [Nishinomiya Shrine](https://nishinomiya-ebisu.com/about/)
- Good relationships, Ōkuninushi no Mikoto (Ōnamuchi no Mikoto). [Kanda Myōjin](https://www.kandamyoujin.or.jp/profile/)
- Medicine and well-being, Sukunahikona no Mikoto. [Kanda Myōjin](https://www.kandamyoujin.or.jp/profile/)
- The sun, Amaterasu Ōmikami. [Ise Jingū](https://www.isejingu.or.jp/about/naiku/)
- Learning, Sugawara no Michizane. [Kitano Tenmangū](https://kitanotenmangu.or.jp/about/)
- Opening the path and safe travel, Sarutahiko Ōkami. [Sarutahiko Shrine](https://www.sarutahikojinja.or.jp/kitou_kigan/)
