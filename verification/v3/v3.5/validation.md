# V3.5.0 validation

Reference-directed illustrated fox loader and procedural mountain waterfall, with a seated star and generated distance-based water sound. The scene is fictional game art inspired by the supplied composition, not an exact real shrine location or photogrammetric reconstruction.

Loader runtime art: 514,556B background,366,172B four-frame fox and204,620B frame; total1,085,348B encoded WebP. The fill and percentage are live weighted startup progress. The browser report samples true startup without stubbing the experience module; progress is monotonic and reaches100%, the bar width matches, and four different fox poses are observed. Reduced-motion preference keeps the loader pose still.

Water: two broad upper/lower volumes plus narrow flow layers, animated procedural noise, broken foam, spray and low mist. No waterfall photograph/video is used. Thirty-eight instanced rocks, static architecture merged by material, shared procedural leaf geometry and a128px×6 static generated forest reflection cube are used. Reflection is illustrative environment lighting, not live SSR or an exact reflected world. Water simulation updates only near the player; the distant geometry remains allocated. Water audio is a generated stereo noise buffer (2,304,000B on this48kHz browser), only allocated after input unlock/approach, then faded/suspended when distant. No claim of total browser RAM/VRAM reduction is made.
